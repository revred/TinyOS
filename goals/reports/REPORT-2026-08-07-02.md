## 1. The result

**The campaign, 6,000 windows of 540,000 physical ticks — 60 s of accumulated window time:**

```text
TOS64-QUAL/1 campaign windows=6000 window_ticks=540000 pmu_per_1000_ticks=44444
TOS64-QUAL/1 campaign_unaccounted min=0 p50=0 p99=0 p99_9=0 max=0
TOS64-QUAL/1 campaign_offset disagreement_max=1
```

**The positive control, the same instrument on the same silicon:**

```text
TOS64-QUAL/1 smc_control psci_version=0x00010001 idle_windows=16
             idle_unaccounted_max=0 control_unaccounted=15
             event_fired=true seen=true
```

Read together, and neither is worth anything alone:

- Over 60 s of accumulated window time the kernel observed **zero** physical ticks its own cycle
  counter could not account for.
- The instrument is **proven able to see** a real EL3 entry: one benign `PSCI_VERSION` SMC — the
  single documented synchronous EL3 entry this platform has (`REPORT-2026-08-07-01` Q2) — cost
  **15 physical ticks**, standing strictly above a floor of **zero** across 16 idle windows, with
  both halves of the verdict printed (`event_fired=true` *and* `seen=true`).
- The **moved-`CNTVOFF_EL2` channel** is clean: `disagreement_max=1` tick, which is read skew
  between two `mrs` instructions, not counter manipulation. It is carried separately because it is
  a different hiding place (`13A`).

**Sensitivity, stated rather than implied.** At 54 MHz, 15 physical ticks is ≈ **278 ns**. The
campaign's floor is therefore not "we saw nothing"; it is "we saw nothing, using an instrument
demonstrated on the same boot to resolve a sub-microsecond EL3 excursion."

## 2. The confound this record nearly filed, and why it is in the Report rather than hidden

The first campaign run — the same fixture, the same 6,000 windows, one line of `config.txt` apart —
produced this:

```text
TOS64-QUAL/1 campaign_unaccounted min=0 p50=0 p99=202499 p99_9=202500 max=202500
```

Read naively that says the secure world stole **3.75 ms of a 10 ms window** in the worst 1% of
windows, which would be a headline finding against `ADR 0005`. **It was not one, and this Report
exists partly to make sure the number is never quoted.**

`202500 / 540000` is exactly `0.375`. `1 − 1500/2400` is exactly `0.375`: the Pi 5's idle core clock
over its boost clock. `PMCCNTR_EL0` counts **CPU cycles**; `CNTPCT_EL0` counts at a **fixed**
frequency; a core at 1500 MHz advances its cycle counter at 62.5% of the calibrated rate, and the
instrument books the shortfall as unaccounted physical ticks — arithmetically indistinguishable from
cycles genuinely spent at EL3.

Two corroborations, both from the same three lines: the distribution was **bimodal with `p50`
exactly 0** (most windows at full clock, a minority at idle clock) rather than the spread a residency
process produces; and `offset_disagreement_max=1` showed no counter being manipulated.

**It cannot be measured around.** Within one window, a core at 62.5% for the whole window and a core
at 100% for 62.5% of it produce identical counter totals. Window arithmetic cannot separate them even
in principle, so the confound was **removed**: `force_turbo=1` now ships in `pi5::CONFIG_TXT`,
globally rather than per-fixture, because the `cycles_per_us=2400` conversion it protects is used by
every measured fixture and not only by the campaign that exposed it.

The two captures are filed side by side deliberately. The unpinned one is evidence about the
*instrument*, and a future reader who sees a large `unaccounted` distribution should suspect the
clock before the secure world.

## 3. Q3's four honest limits

This record is quotable only within these, and each is a real narrowing:

1. **It is a record about a pinned clock.** The campaign ran with `force_turbo=1`. It does not
   license a claim about a board running an ordinary DVFS governor, and `LE-121`'s open half is that
   nothing *checks* the clock was pinned — `config.txt` lives on a card, the same class of constant
   no test can reach that the divergence record's `os_check=0` lesson is about.
2. **It is a record about one core.** Cores 1–3 park in `wfe` (`boot.rs`, `FEAT-P1-07` §6). EL3
   behaviour with four cores active is unmeasured, and nothing here predicts it.
3. **It is a record about 60 s.** The duration is stated because the ADR requires it stated. A
   longer campaign could still find a rarer event; "zero over 60 s at 278 ns resolution" is the
   claim, and "never" is not.
4. **It is a record about a near-idle system.** The board ran the park loop. Q2 established that
   nothing asynchronous routes to EL3 on this platform, which is why an idle campaign is meaningful
   at all — but a loaded system was not measured.

## 4. What this closes, and what it does not

**Closes:** `ADR 0005` Q3 for `rpi5-bcm2712`, within §3's limits. With Q1 and Q2 held and Q4 stated
in `REPORT-2026-08-07-01`, **all four parts of the qualification record now exist**, and
`qualified-platforms.tsv` moves `rpi5-bcm2712` from `unqualified` to `qualified` citing this Report.

**Does not close:** any `G04` gate. A qualified platform makes `G04` *reachable*; each gate still
needs its own measured evidence under real-time conditions, and this Report measures none of them.
The distinction is `ADR 0015`'s: TinyOS declares and enforces a budget, and a qualification record
is what makes a measured bound quotable at all — it is not itself a bound.

**Does not close `LE-121`.** The confound is removed and proven removed; the *refusal* is not built.
The campaign still emits a plausible distribution on an unpinned card with nothing marking it, and
the derivable guard — the rate calibration's own per-window samples are bimodal when the clock moves
and constant when it does not — uses data the instrument already collects.

**Does not close `LE-120`.** `write_campaign_line` is guarded; every other `TOS64` producer is not,
and the `METRIC` rows are the longest lines this project emits.

## 5. How the evidence was produced, because the method is the finding

Four boots, all four power-cycled by software, each `xtask board-run --plug=… --dialect=shelly-gen2`:
serve the image over DHCP+TFTP with the digest verified before execution, cut mains, watch the wire
for a named condition, harvest, and **leave the board ON whatever happened** — a clause exercised for
real, since the final `parse-meas` step failed on the campaign runs (the qual fixture emits `QUAL`
lines, not a `MEAS` envelope) and the board stayed powered every time.

Each boot cost one command and roughly 78 s. That is the whole reason this Report has a §2: **the
second run existed only because the first found a defect**, and a bench where re-running is free is a
bench where a confound surfaces in five minutes instead of being filed as a result.
