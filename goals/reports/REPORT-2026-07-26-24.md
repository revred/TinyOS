**What changed:** `exec::shared_memory::grant` is now fully transactional (zero-page requests rejected outright; a mid-loop mapping failure or a full `GrantRegistry` rolls back every page already mapped by that call) and generation-safe (`GrantRegistry<CAPACITY>` stamps each successful grant with a monotonically increasing generation; `revoke` rejects a stale token — one whose `sharee_virt` a different, later grant now occupies — with `SharedMemoryError::StaleGrant` instead of silently tearing down the wrong grant). `fixture_shared_memory_main.rs` was updated to thread a static `GrantRegistry` through its existing grant/revoke calls; its own asserted behavior is unchanged.
