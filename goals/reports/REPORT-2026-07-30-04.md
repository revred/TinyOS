## What this report claims

1. **The `motion` crate exists and is contract-complete for `MFS-01`.** Typed
   group/axis/feedback/epoch/time identities (constructors refuse out-of-range,
   wrap-aware epoch stepping), fixed-capacity `FeedbackFrame<32>` /
   `ActuationFrame<16>` with whole-group validity masks, a declared `GroupProfile`,
   whole-epoch feedback validation and whole-frame staging validation with one typed
   rejection per disposition arm, the narrow `MotionGroupTransport` contract
   (single-use move-consumed `CommitToken`), and the deterministic in-memory double.
2. **TDD held.** The complete 51-test suite was written first and observed failing as a
   119-error compile-stage Red (`cargo test -p motion`, no implementation line
   present); it went Green without a test changing.
3. **Every safety detector has a positive control.** Missing mandatory feedback, each
   of the six non-valid quality states, repeated epoch, out-of-order epoch (ahead and
   behind), wrong group, both identity-mismatch shapes, incomplete staging, apply-epoch
   retag, epoch-mismatched commit, late commit, commit-before-timeline and
   script-capacity exhaustion are each driven deliberately, and the stage/commit arms
   are proven by observing the double's staged/committed record, not by trusting the
   returned error.
4. **The no-allocation claim is compiler-shaped, not asserted.** The crate is
   `no_std` outside `cfg(test)`, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, declares no allocator, and
   was added to `xtask`'s `SHIPPED_CRATES` no-heap gate, so an allocator ever appearing
   withdraws the evidence loudly.
