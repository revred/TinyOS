**Deliberately not done:**

- **No preemptive dispatch.** This dispatcher is cooperative: a task must call `context::switch` back itself to yield; nothing forces a task that never yields to give up control. True preemption needs a timer interrupt / IDT this kernel doesn't have yet (`STORY-P0-05-02`'s named gap) — restated here as this Story's own explicit limit, not silently assumed solved by adding a ready-queue.
- **Does not drive `kernel::wcet::record_tick`.** This dispatch loop is not a periodic timer source; `STORY-P0-02-04`'s own gap (no live timer, no real watchdog) is independent of this Story and remains fully open — a dispatch loop existing doesn't imply a timer exists to call it on a schedule.
- **No automatic task-state transitions on lock contention.** `crate::lock::PriorityInheritingLock::try_lock` still doesn't itself mark a contending task `Blocked` — this Story's own test does that explicitly, by design (`crate::lock`'s own scope note: blocking a task is the caller's responsibility). A future Story could integrate the two more tightly if warranted.
- **No multi-task round-robin fairness policy beyond "highest priority wins."** Two Ready tasks at the *same* priority are broken by `Iterator::max_by_key`'s documented last-wins tie-break (most recently created), not a rotating fairness scheme — acceptable for this Story's scope (proving priority-ordered selection works, including after a boost), not a claim about fairness among equal-priority tasks.
