- Caught during implementation: an initial version of `revoke` took its `SharedGrant` argument by value. A rejected (wrong-caller) revoke attempt would have consumed and dropped the caller's only token regardless of the `Err` result — permanently losing the real owner's own ability to ever revoke a live grant, since nothing else holds it. Fixed by taking `grant: &SharedGrant` (borrowed) instead, before landing; `revocation_by_a_non_owner_is_rejected` asserts the mapping survives specifically to catch a regression of this.
