**`LE-09` — no hardware tier — is the correct blocker for exactly one of twenty-five.** `G08` wants retired-instruction, branch-miss and L1D-miss counts, and QEMU/TCG exposes no PMU. Every other blocker in this domain is tooling, environment, or a subsystem that does not exist yet — none of which a Raspberry Pi 5 arriving in the post would change.

This is `LE-31`'s hypothesis, evidenced for one domain: the project-wide attribution of `0 / 57 Stories assurance-verified` to `LE-09` is wrong here in 24 cases out of 25.

## The finding, registered as `LE-42`

**The accept path measures 17.6–39.1× over every latency and cycle budget `D09` states.**

Stated with its limits, because the limits are large: TCG does not model cache and its timing is not proportional to hardware, so the magnitude does not transfer; and a run-to-run p99 CV of 22–71% against `G05`'s ≤ 5% requirement means **no stable verdict exists in this environment at all**. `G05` failing is the reason the other six cannot be evaluated, not a separate defect.

What survives those caveats: a 30× overshoot is well outside what measurement noise plausibly explains, and the cost of parsing a PE64 image had never been measured before this run. `LE-42` asks for a re-measurement on a stable environment (a CI run, per `LE-23`) before any conclusion about the parser is drawn.

## Provenance note

The gated timing baseline was **not** rewritten, and `--update-baseline` was not run (`LE-28`). `pe-measure` sits outside the gated `measure` envelope, following `fixture-pool-bench`, because the gate refuses a measured-but-unbaselined metric and a same-commit re-record taken on this Windows dev host would bake in the confirmed 23–53% cross-host offset (`LE-23`).

`D09`'s measured evidence was blocked by baseline provenance, not by hardware. For an audit whose subject is *what actually blocks `verified`*, that is arguably the more useful of the two findings.
