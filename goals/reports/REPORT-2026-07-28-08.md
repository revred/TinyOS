## The evidence, and why it is stronger than the guardrail asks

`G11`'s target is *heap allocations per steady-state work unit = 0; pool claims are separately counted.*

**There is no heap.** Every shipped crate — `hal`, `hal-arm64`, `hal-x86_64`, `exec`, `kernel`, `os` — declares `#![no_std]`, none declares a `#[global_allocator]`, and none names `extern crate alloc` or `use alloc::` outside `#[cfg(test)]`. In Rust that is not an observation about a benchmark run; it is **enforced by the compiler**, because a `no_std` crate without a global allocator cannot use `alloc` and fails to build if it tries.

So the recorded result is stronger than the guardrail's own wording: **zero heap allocations in every state**, not merely in steady state, on every architecture, under every load, with no measurement uncertainty and no hardware dependency.

Static pool claims are unaffected and remain separately counted, exactly as the target text requires — `D07` exists to measure them and is not touched by this.

## The gate was proved able to fail

Per this repository's standing discipline — the one `fixture-broken-boot` established and `STORY-P0-01-04` found nine violations of — the check was deliberately falsified before being trusted. Appending `use alloc::boxed::Box;` to `os/src/kernel/src/lib.rs`:

```text
xtask: assurance spine invalid: no-heap gate: os/src/kernel/src/lib.rs:27 contains `use alloc::`
outside `#[cfg(test)]`; every `PERF-Dnn-G11` row in guardrail-evidence.tsv rests on this system
having no heap, so add an allocator only by withdrawing that evidence first
```

Reverted; green again. The message names the consequence rather than the rule, so whoever trips it learns what it costs.

It was included on the strength of the subsystem existing, but the catalogue's readiness field has drifted and that is worth a pass of its own.

## What this does **not** establish

- **No Story became `verified`, and the dashboard's `0 / 56` is unchanged.** Ten gates of **391** in play (17 selected domains × 23 release gates). The Story-level zero is joined by a gate-level count, never replaced by it — replacing an uncomfortable true signal with a friendlier one is the failure this Story exists to avoid.
- **`G09` (image and feature footprint) is not claimed**, though its cadence is also every-PR. Its method is a per-feature section-size delta against the parent commit; `check-image-size` enforces one whole-image ceiling. Different measurement. Claiming the gate on the strength of a related one is the substitution this project refuses elsewhere.
- **`G21` is not claimed** — its every-PR half is fault containment, its other half is timing.
- **Nothing about latency, cycles, tails, throughput, soak, or isolation.** Those are `LE-09`'s, and this Story does not touch them.
- **The register is hand-written.** No tooling derives rows from Reports, so a Report can still land without its gates being recorded. Named as this Story's own debt.
