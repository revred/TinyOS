  1. 01ACover note: plan TinyTile, the heterogeneous compute access layer

    Commissions — and scopes — the session that plans TinyTile: a Rust, C-ABI, native-fast compute-access layer letting AI and similar throughput workloads reach a GPU or accelerator, inspired by TileLang’s tile-level programming model. Four owner-settled constraints are recorded as closed. The mandate’s centre is the collision nobody has ruled on yet: a GPU kernel is executable code for a bus-mastering processor, TileLang natively reaches the device through Python→TVM→nvcc JIT, and RCG-11 denies JIT by name — three candidate shapes are tabled, with ahead-of-time off-target compilation recommended and an ADR required either way. Second-order: PD-10’s “IOMMU or wiped bounce buffer” is in direct tension with G-HW-6’s zero-copy promise; the vendor-driver question (“consume CUDA” cannot mean “run NVIDIA’s Linux driver”) must be answered or recorded as unknown. Fills the EPIC-P6B slot that has sat in the backlog with no Epic document. Explicitly not a design and not an implementation.
